# PanelBreak — Audio Cue Sheet

Sound is half the energy. Keep it consistent across every upload so viewers
recognize the channel with their eyes closed.

## Intro (6s) — SFX timeline
| Time | Cue | Source / search term |
|------|-----|----------------------|
| 00:00 | Sub rumble, building | "cinematic sub rumble riser" |
| 00:01 | Rising whoosh | "whoosh riser transition" |
| 00:02 | **BASS HIT + ink splat** (the slam) | "cinematic boom impact" + "ink splat" |
| 00:03.8 | Page flip + key clicks (per char) | "paper flip" + "mechanical key click" |
| 00:05 | Snap + short tail reverb | "snap transition" |

- Loudness: **−14 LUFS** integrated · true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
- The bass hit at 00:02 is the ONLY peak — everything else sits under it.

## Outro (20s) — music
- One mid-energy loop that **resolves on the final downbeat** (~00:19.5). No fade-under-talk.
- Duck music −6 dB under the spoken line during Beat 02.
- Reuse the same loop every video.

## Voice (faceless narration)
- **VO line (outro):** "That's the arc — broken down. Tap subscribe, and I'll see you in the next panel."
- Tone: confident, warm, a little fast — energetic but not shouty.
- Chain: high-pass @ 80Hz → de-ess → gentle compression (3:1) → −16 LUFS for spoken sections.
- If using TTS: pick one voice and never change it — the voice IS the brand.
